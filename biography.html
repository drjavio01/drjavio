Biography

El Rey Gay Del Cuba — La historia detrás de la leyenda

Javiero Josias Fernández

Known to the world as Dr Javio

Born on December 21, 2004, in the vibrant city of Cienfuegos, Cuba, Javiero Josias Fernández — known to the world as Dr Javio — emerged as a revolutionary force in Latin music. As the first openly gay Cuban artist in Reggaeton music history, he shattered barriers and redefined what it means to be authentic in an industry that has long demanded conformity.

Now based in the United States, Dr Javio carries the spirit of Cuba in every beat, blending the raw energy of Reggaeton with deeply personal lyrics that speak to identity, love, rebellion, and resilience. Known by many names — El Rey Gay Del Cuba, Diosa de amor, La Chingona, La Serpiente Roja, La imparable — each title reflects a chapter of his journey and a facet of his unapologetic artistry.

The Journey

2004
Born in Cienfuegos, Cuba — a city of music, culture, and resilience. From an early age, Javiero was drawn to rhythm and performance, finding his voice through the vibrant sounds of Cuban streets.
2018
Began writing his first songs, channeling personal experiences of identity, struggle, and pride into lyrics that would later define a new wave in Cuban music.
2020
Adopted the name Dr Javio and began releasing music independently, quickly building an underground following across Cuba and the Latin diaspora online.
2022
Made the bold move to the United States, carrying his Cuban roots and musical vision to a global stage. Began recording professional tracks and developing his signature sound.
2024
At just 21 years old, Dr Javio solidified his position as the first openly gay Cuban Reggaeton artist, earning recognition as El Rey Gay Del Cuba. His music continues to break boundaries and inspire a generation.
2025
Preparing new music releases and expanding his reach globally. The journey of La Serpiente Roja is just beginning — unstoppable, unapologetic, and unforgettable.
